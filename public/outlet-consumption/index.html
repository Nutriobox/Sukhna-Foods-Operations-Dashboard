Source: computed from Opening + Purchase + Consumption + Variance
Formula. Everything is normalised to each RM's L1 base unit so the arithmetic is apples-to-apples.
Ideal Closing (L1) = Opening (L1) + Purchase (L1) − Consumption (L1) − Variance (L1). Consumption per RM = Σ (item qty sold × BOM raw qty per item, converted to L1) across every KOT in the window. The Full and Loose columns on the right split the closing figure using the Loose Denomination mark for that RM (Loose = remainder below one full pack in the loose UOM; Full = integer packs at the next level up).
Status. Consumption pulls from PetPooja (live); Opening, Purchase and Variance are editable placeholder cells for now and will plug into the Opening Inventory save, the Purchase (GRN) sync and a wastage source as those backends land.
