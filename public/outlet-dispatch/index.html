Stationary & Small ware

Stationary and Small ware are orders, not stock counts — whatever Order Qty an outlet enters on these two tabs is sent as it is. Both are read in automatically (blue); click a list to check it, or type over any quantity here and that is what gets sent. Everything asked for goes on the order sheet and the Dry Store / PM / HK challan. Only items that exist in the PACT Product Master also go into the PACT import; the rest are marked challan only and listed in the Validation Report.

★

Product Master links

which master row each product goes into PACT as

Several master rows share a Product Name, so matching by name alone can send PACT the wrong product. Every product on the Ideal Inventory is listed with the master row it will go to; pick a different row once and every future run uses it. Links are kept in this browser — export them to move to another machine.
